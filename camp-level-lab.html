Camp levels

The camp grows from a fire to a little home. A level is a place you build (its keystone), not a number you reach. Raising it widens the ring, does one real thing, and opens one or two more things to make. Whatever you make is made by you: the camp never fills itself with things you didn't build.

      How it works the proposal, after review

      Cosy is a list of deeds

      Every point has a name and is earned once, so it can't be farmed and the card can show what made it. Cosy never goes down. Raising the camp takes 8 · 18 · 28 · 40; the deeds below reach 52 before level V, twelve to spare, alone.

      The ring

      CAMP_R is 5 today. It stays the floor: 5 → 5.5 → 6 → 6.5 → 7 tiles. A camp never shrinks, so nothing placed today ends up outside its ring.

      Kept meadows open at level I with the deeds they've already done counted (camp made, basket, seat, torches), so an old camp may find itself a few points from Hearth on its first visit.

      The ground shader's camp ellipse grows over the moment and then stays still: it repaints the ground only while it spreads.

      One real payoff a level

      • I · home: you wake here after a collapse (already true).
      • II · the hearthstones hold the heat: a stick burns a third longer (FIRE_STICK).
      • III · baking at the oven, which is already a STATIONS entry.
      • IV · the lantern arch: the ring keeps a steady light at night (a torch's pool, never burns out).
      • V · the cabin: dry and warm inside, and where you wake. Needs one new item, logs.

      Keystones II–IV cost only things that lie about already (pebbles, clay, sticks, bark).
