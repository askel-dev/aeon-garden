🎧 Garden Sound Lab

A quiet sound palette for the meadow, made on the fly with no audio files. Every pitched sound uses one pentatonic scale. Rabbits play a high kalimba, foxes a low wooden marimba, and the meadow itself rings a soft bell.

🔊

"Let the meadow play" runs a 20-second day like the game at Calm speed and triggers events about as often as a real meadow does.

Birds sing by day with a dawn chorus. Crickets and the odd owl come out at night. The wind comes in gusts that swell, cross from one side to the other and rustle the grass (dry leaves in autumn); in winter and storms a hard gust whistles faintly, in tune. Winter has a few snowy chimes. Rain and storms hiss and drip, fog and snow muffle everything, a heatwave brings cicadas, and a wildfire crackles. Bees flying on screen add a faint hum in tune. At 15× and 60×, day and night blur into one steady mix, and only the big moments (seasons, newcomers, extinctions) still make a sound.

How it's put together

  • One scale, three voices. D major pentatonic only, so any two sounds that overlap still sound nice together.
  • Rising means life, falling means loss. Love and births go up. Catches and deaths go down, softly, with no stings or screams.
  • Quiet by default. Each sound has a cooldown, and there are at most about 6 animal sounds a second. Sounds far from the camera come out quieter and with more echo.
  • The clock is the score. The background follows the time of day, the season and the weather, so a year in the meadow has its own sound.
