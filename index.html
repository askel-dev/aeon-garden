🌱 Welcome to AEON Garden

A small meadow where rabbits and foxes live their own lives. Nobody tells them what to do. They graze, run for their burrows, fall in love and raise families. Every baby inherits a mix of its parents' traits, with a small twist.

Leave it running and watch what happens: populations boom and crash, and over the years the animals slowly change.
