A craft button

One tactile button for making a thing at the fire. Parchment and brass like the rest of the game, sized for a thumb, and it tells you what it will cost before you press it.

The button at 62 px, with its parts named

44 pxthe HUD, a phone corner

Anatomy

Clay fill
The warm gradient only when you have everything: the one loud thing on the screen.
Brass plate
An inked picture on a little stamped plate. Here: a pot, with a spark for “made”.
Label & cost
What you'll make, then what it takes in beads. Costs drop off on narrow phones; the label keeps the count.
Extruded foot
A 4 px bottom edge. Pressing pushes it 3 px down and shrinks the edge to 1 px: it moves like an object.
Bloom
A slow brass halo, 2.6 s, only while it's ready. Nothing blinks for attention.

The states ready · short · working · locked · just made

Ready

Clay fill, brass rim, a slow bloom.

Short

Quiet parchment. The line under the name says what's short.

Working

No pressing. A bar creeps along its foot.

Locked

The padlock says why, in one short line.

Just made

One pale gleam across it, then it settles.

In the meadow a strip at the foot, in thumb's reach

Day 3 · 1×

Sits beside the satchel, in reach of a thumb, and reads as the loudest thing in the strip only when it can be pressed.

Rules it follows the parts you don't see

44 px, always

The small round one is 44 × 44; the bar is 52–62 tall. A thumb reaches it at the foot of an upright phone.

Costs before the press

The beads are the satchel's own words (3 clay, 1 stick), so there's no second window to open to find out.

Pressed, not hovered

Hover only brightens 3%. The press moves it 3 px into its brass foot, which is the game's usual feel.

Never a spinner

Working is a bar that fills and a pot that breathes. Finite, calm, and it never asks for another press.

One accent

Only the ready state carries the clay fill and the bloom. Short, locked and working all fall back to parchment.

Painted, like the rest

Opaque parchment, no blur behind it; the grain is one SVG layer. The bar animates width on a 5 px strip, nothing full-screen.
